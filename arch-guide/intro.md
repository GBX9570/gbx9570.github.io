# Introduction to Arch Linux
I think its safe to assume you've finally decided to dip your toes into the lovechild of Richard Stallman and Linus Torvalds, and if you haven't, you're probably planning on it soon. Well this is your one stop shop for the basics of Arch Linux (and in some ways, Linux overall). In this guide, you'll get taught all the basics of Linux and how to get around.

# Why should I use Linux?
Linux is an incredibly versatile operating system which you can bend to do literally anything. if you want it to look pink and sparkly you can do that. If you want a million black and white terminals, you can do that. If you want it to look like a mac or a windows computer you can also do that. It can also be made to work for basically any usecass - some with more pain to get working than others, but it works nonetheless. Linux is what YOU make of it, not what a company makes of it.
